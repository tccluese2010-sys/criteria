FAQ

Questions answered.

Is the preview really free? +
Yes — completely. We invest time creating a watermarked preview for every approved request. You only pay once you've seen it and decided you love it.
What if I don't like the preview? +
No problem. Tell us what to change and we'll revise the preview. If after revisions it's still not right, you walk away — no charge, no hard feelings.
How long does a preview take? +
Most graphics and posters are previewed within 24–48 hours. Websites and video edits may take 3–5 days for a quality preview depending on scope.
What formats are final files delivered in? +
Posters and logos: PNG, PDF, SVG. Videos: MP4 (1080p or 4K). Websites: full source code or deployed link. Everything you need to use the work immediately.
How do I pay after approval? +
We'll send you a UPI link or Razorpay payment request. Simple, fast, and secure. No complex checkout needed.
Do you work with international clients? +
Yes. We accept international payments via bank transfer or card. Pricing will be quoted in your preferred currency upon request.
